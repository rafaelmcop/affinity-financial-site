Connect your authorized Five Rings data

This extension reads client records, policy information and available production or credit figures from your signed-in Five Rings account and transfers them to your signed-in Affinity agent portal.

Records can include names, contact details, dates of birth, policy numbers, premiums and other information displayed in client or policy details. Authorized Affinity administrators and assigned assistants may access imported data according to portal permissions.

Read progress can be kept temporarily in this extension's session memory to resume an interrupted capture. The extension does not extract passwords, verification codes or cookies. You complete login and security checks directly with Five Rings.

Synchronization may run in the background while your authorized portal session is active. This is an independent Affinity integration, not a Five Rings product or endorsement.

Privacy policy and support

Turn off this option to stop new sync jobs. An in-progress read may finish. Remove the extension to stop all extension activity.
