Affinity Five Rings Connector Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Purpose and authorization

The connector is an independent Affinity Financial Consulting integration. It reads information from an authorized user's signed-in Five Rings browser session and transfers it to that user's authenticated Affinity agent portal. It is not offered, endorsed or approved by Five Rings Financial. Chrome Web Store distribution does not establish such an affiliation.

Information handled

Depending on information available in the Five Rings account, captures may include agent identity, client names and contact information, dates of birth, policy identifiers, premiums, coverage, policy status, production or credit figures, and other information displayed in client or policy details. Policy details may contain sensitive financial or underwriting information. The connector reads only the permitted Five Rings pages and the Affinity portal needed for synchronization.

Credentials and security checks

Users sign in directly with Five Rings and complete any security verification there. The connector uses the browser's existing authenticated requests; it does not extract or transmit passwords, authentication cookies or verification codes. It does not bypass login, MFA or reCAPTCHA.

Use and access

Information is used to synchronize the user's Affinity client, policy and production records. Authorized Affinity administrators and assigned assistants can access imported records according to portal access controls. Data is sent over HTTPS to www.affinityfc.org, whose infrastructure is operated using Cloudflare. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or transferred for creditworthiness or lending decisions. The extension does not add advertising or analytics scripts. Affinity hosting systems may keep operational and security logs.

Temporary browser storage

The store-distributed connector keeps a consent preference in local extension storage. Read checkpoints are kept in extension session memory, separate from passwords and cookies. A checkpoint is eligible for resumption for up to ten minutes, is scoped to the configured account, and is cleared after completion or when a later read detects an expired or different-account checkpoint. Session checkpoints are also cleared when the extension is reloaded, disabled, updated or the browser restarts. This checkpoint data is not stored in Chrome sync storage.

Portal retention and deletion

Records successfully imported into Affinity remain in the portal until an authorized administrator removes them under the organization's recordkeeping requirements. Removing the extension or disabling synchronization does not delete records already imported. To request access, correction or deletion, contact Affinity using the address below.

Your controls

The store-distributed extension asks you to review and enable data access before new synchronization jobs can start. Use the connector icon to disable new jobs; an existing read may finish. Uninstall the extension to stop all extension activity. Closing your Five Rings session may require you to reconnect later. You must be authorized to transfer the records and should use separate browser profiles for different Five Rings users.
