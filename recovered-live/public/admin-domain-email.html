Corporate portal senders

Using Gmail, Outlook or another mail app

Replies sent from a personal account do not pass through the Affinity portal and cannot be converted after sending. To show a corporate sender in a mail app, configure an authorized corporate account or “Send mail as” in that app. Forwarding alone only handles incoming mail.
