Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · Version 2026-11-23.1

Affinity Financial Consulting Inc. operates this website and Affinity Portal. Contact info@affinityfc.org or write to 247 Washington St, Stoughton, MA, United States, for privacy requests and security concerns.

Information processed

Depending on the features used, we process account and professional details; client and beneficiary identity and contact details; appointments; applications and policy information; income and payment details; health and underwriting information; submitted identity documents; messages, attachments and contact-source information; reviews; permission changes and security/audit records. Sensitive application fields and credentials are subject to additional server-side protection. Do not submit information beyond what the service requires.

Purposes and access

We use information to operate accounts, prepare and follow up on applications, maintain client records, deliver requested communications and reminders, provide support, prevent misuse and meet applicable obligations. The responsible professional, explicitly assigned assistants and authorized platform administrators may access relevant records for these purposes. Administrative audit access can include account records and activity. Unrelated professionals are restricted from one another's records unless access is explicitly assigned through the portal.

Connected accounts and imported contacts

Connecting email or calendar accounts allows the selected features to read, organize or send authorized data. OAuth authorization takes place with the provider; application passwords may be used for supported services. Tokens and application passwords are encrypted server-side. When WhatsApp contact import is enabled, available group, conversation and contact records may be imported, including names, telephone numbers, group sources and importing-user attribution. Priority selection affects contact order, while administrative assignment and exclusions determine workflow access.

Providers and transfers

Cloudflare provides hosting, D1 storage, network security and Workers AI. Google Fonts may deliver website fonts and receive technical connection data. Enabled integrations may involve Google, Microsoft, Apple/iCloud, Calendly, WhatsApp, National Life, Five Rings and the user's chosen email providers. Video playback can connect to Vimeo or YouTube after the visitor chooses to load an external player. These providers may process IP addresses, device information, cookies and account data according to their own policies. Processing may occur in the United States or other locations used by a provider. Access is limited by the configured purpose and permissions.

Google API Limited Use

Google mailbox and Calendar data is used for the user-facing features authorized by the account holder. Calendar authorization is separate from Gmail authorization. Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements, applies. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. Revoke access through the provider's account controls or disconnect the integration in the portal.

Cookies, analytics and external media

Essential cookies support sessions, security and versioned acceptance records. We do not deploy advertising pixels or marketing analytics SDKs in the application code covered by this release. Cloudflare may process technical traffic and security signals to provide the service. External media is optional and remains unloaded until selected. Privacy controls let you withdraw permission for future external-player loading. Necessary service cookies do not authorize marketing tracking. New optional tracking requires a separate review and appropriate choices.

AI choices and no model training

The interactive AI assistant is optional. AI processing and conversation-memory choices are presented separately during registration and may later be changed voluntarily in Privacy settings. The assistant remains disabled until AI processing is explicitly enabled. No agreement or permission window is opened automatically during portal use. The portal does not fine-tune models or use user information to train models. Cloudflare states that Workers AI customer content is not used to train AI models. Optional conversation memory and writing preferences require a separate opt-in. They provide contextual continuity rather than model training. With memory disabled, new prompts and answers are not saved as AI conversation history by this feature; minimal usage and security metadata may remain. Previously stored history is not reused while memory is disabled. Do not enter passwords, verification codes, full account numbers or unnecessary medical details in AI prompts. Client context is scoped to the authenticated account and delegated permissions. Requested document-reading and translation workflows may also process the submitted documents or text with Cloudflare; this service processing does not train models.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the record type, configured workflow and applicable obligations. External-professional draft applications are scheduled for deletion after seven days from creation. Following completion and document submission, those applications and documents are delivered to the completing professional and info@affinityfc.org and scheduled for deletion from the active portal database within 72 hours. Email copies remain in the recipient mailboxes under their retention policies. Short-lived WhatsApp message/media retention and important communication audit history follow the portal's configured retention. Acceptance, security and necessary business records may be retained to document obligations and investigate incidents. Backups and provider retention can delay complete erasure. Ask us about a particular record.

Acceptance and preferences records

We record the server time, document versions and hashes, acceptance purpose, authenticated account when available, and a pseudonymous browser receipt. We use a keyed hash of the network address and a bounded technical browser description for accountability, avoiding storage of the raw IP in the consent ledger. Optional media, AI processing and AI memory choices are recorded separately from acceptance of service terms. You may update optional choices through Privacy settings.

Security and incidents

We use HTTPS, restricted server-side database bindings, encrypted storage provided by Cloudflare, password hashing, encryption for sensitive application fields and integration secrets, account permissions and administrative logs. No system can eliminate every risk. Report suspected exposure immediately to info@affinityfc.org. We investigate, contain incidents, preserve relevant evidence and alert the responsible administrator. Notifications to affected individuals, regulators and authorities are made according to applicable requirements and the verified facts; an ordinary service error alone is not a finding of a data breach.

Requests and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or information about sharing and may withdraw optional permissions. We verify identity before disclosing or changing sensitive records. Some records must remain for legal, security or business obligations, which we will explain. For marketing messages, use available opt-out mechanisms or contact us. The portal is intended for adults and authorized professionals; information about children should be submitted only by an authorized adult for a legitimate service purpose.
