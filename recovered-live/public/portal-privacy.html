Privacy and data handling

Affinity Portal provides professional client records, applications, appointments and communication tools. Each professional uses an individual account. Platform administrators can access records for operation, information and auditing; assigned assistants can access only the functions and owners explicitly authorized.

Email connections

Each user connects their own email account. Google or Microsoft authorization is performed directly with that provider when the integration is available. Access and refresh tokens and manually supplied application passwords are encrypted server-side. Tokens are not shown to other agents. Authorized email access is used to send and receive messages and organize the connected user's mailbox within the portal. Access is not sold or used to train generalized AI models.

By connecting a mailbox, the user authorizes the portal to process its messages for these features and acknowledges administrative auditing of portal records. Google user data is handled under the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Users can revoke provider authorization in their Google or Microsoft account and ask the platform administrator to disconnect the portal.

Google Calendar connections

Users authorize Google Calendar directly with Google and select one professional calendar to display in the portal. Read-only access is used to list calendars and import appointment times, titles, participants and meeting links. This connection does not grant access to Gmail or change events in Google. Tokens are encrypted separately from mailbox credentials. Imported appointments and client records are visible only to the account owner, authorized assigned assistants and platform administrators for operation and auditing. Disconnecting removes the stored Google Calendar tokens; existing appointment and client history is preserved. Google Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements apply.

Application retention for external professionals

Draft applications are retained for up to seven days from creation. Once completed, the application and uploaded documents are sent from an Affinity email address to the professional who completed the form and to info@affinityfc.org. The application and its stored document payloads are scheduled for deletion from the active portal database within 72 hours after completion. Copies delivered by email remain in the recipient mailboxes, subject to their own retention policies. Minimal processing and deletion audit records can remain.

Reviews

Completed applications can generate an email requesting a client review and recommendation. Review records identify the professional internally for auditing. Publication and public display of the professional's name are controlled by platform administrators.

Access and contact

For account access, data requests, deletion or connection revocation assistance, contact info@affinityfc.org.
