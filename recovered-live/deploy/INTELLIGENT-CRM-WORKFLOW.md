# Application and confirmed-communication CRM projection

A saved application creates/links an owned client using explicit origin meeting
or the strict person resolver. A shared telephone never identifies a person.
Only missing basic fields are filled; conflicting birth dates require review.
No SSN, bank, medical or document payload is copied into CRM notes.
Submission projects application_submitted, without inventing an issued policy.
A real linked policy controls InForce/Lapsada and confirmed premiums/coverage.

Confirmed proposal/accompaniment templates project Follow-up; confirmed absence
notices project No-show. A draft, open-app action, failed or unconfirmed dispatch
never triggers this rule. Unknown/custom message text is not interpreted as a
business outcome. Each owner/meeting/result has one durable event; combined
email/WhatsApp sends cannot duplicate the transition. The minute job retries
pending business synchronization without resending the communication. Existing
follow-up dates survive; no date is fabricated. Policies, submitted applications,
terminal decisions and custom stages are protected from older message events.

Email PDF facts use the existing archive reader. Confirmed WhatsApp proposal PDFs
are parsed in the request's background lifetime, without retaining another file
payload beyond the existing WhatsApp retention policy. Only an identified insured
and an explicit valid DOB can fill an empty field. Relative/beneficiary dates,
multiple people or conflicting DOBs require review. English labels use US dates;
Portuguese DOB labels use day/month. Age alone never becomes a DOB.

# Automatic WhatsApp delivery policy

Only meeting reminders (24 hours / 30 minutes) and birthdays are allowed.
Legacy generic automation permission cannot authorize campaigns. Server-side
SQLite quota reserves at most five automated attempts total per physical WhatsApp
number and New York date, across both permitted types. Manual sends are separate.
Reservations remain consumed on uncertainty, persist across restarts, and dedupe
birthday phone/day or meeting/stage/phone/day. Stale birthdays and expired meeting
notices are refused; quota/duplicate refusals are never retried as service-busy.
The portal runner independently excludes every non-birthday campaign regardless
of a stale enabled setting. Birthday identity and opt-out guards still apply.
The paused hnempreendedor@gmail.com account remains paused.

Enabling allowed birthday delivery does not reconnect or unblock a banned phone.
No live test message is needed to validate quota or CRM/PDF projection rules.
